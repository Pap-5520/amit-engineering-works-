Tungsten Carbide Tamping Tool — Specifications

  • Manufactured from Chromium Molybdenum / Nickel Chromium Molybdenum alloy steel
  • Withstands minimum vibration frequency of 35 Hz and squeeze pressure of 120 kg/sq.cm
  • CNC-produced matching fitting surfaces; full tungsten carbide coverage on pad and wear areas
  • Slim, insertion-friendly profile meeting RDSO specification
  • Saves minimum 30% cost vs ordinary tamping tools; increases tool life up to 15×
  • Reduces transportation time and manpower by up to 20×
  • Compatible with Plassrer 09/32 CSM, 09/16 CSM, 08/32U, 08/32, 07/16, 09-Dynamic, Duomatic, Unimate 3S & 4S and others

Squeezing Cylinders — Specifications

  • Conforms to IS: 2062-1992; minimum tensile strength 410 MPa (N/sq.mm); elongation ≥ 23%
  • Non-functional surface: golden yellow synthetic enamel (two coats); functional surface: boiled linseed oil per IS: 771976
  • Oil holes protected with plastic caps; manufacturer's code engraved on each cylinder
  • 09-CSM: 50% higher work capacity, non-synchronous uniform pressure tamping, 35 Hz optimum frequency, reduced wear
  • 09-3X: 30–40% output increase vs two-sleeper machines; flexible single or three-sleeper tamping; ideal for short track possession windows

Cutting Chain for Ballast Cleaning — Specifications

  • Chain consists of scraper shovels with 2–5 carbide fingers, connecting links and bolts
  • Guided underneath the track in a cutter bar for exact, straight cuts
  • Chain guide can be displaced laterally on curves without affecting formation crossfall or cleaning depth
  • Possible excavating depth: up to 900 mm (RM 76 U) and 1150 mm (RM 80 U)
  • Required subgrade crossfall can be produced accurately during work

TC Finger Chains — Specifications

  • Five easily exchangeable scraper fingers designed as armoured tungsten carbide round shaft chisels
  • Fully compliant for excavating, measuring, control system, screening, ballasting and spoil evacuation
  • Compatible with standard machines RM 92 U and RM 80
  • Meets all expectations for high-quality ballast cleaning requirements
